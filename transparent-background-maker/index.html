Transparent images explained

Remove the scene and keep only the portrait.

A transparent image has invisible pixels around the visible subject instead of a solid rectangular background. It can be placed over a colour, page, slide or other photograph without showing the original scene.

PNG is the correct download format for transparency. JPEG does not support transparent pixels and will replace them with a solid colour. PixEdito therefore exports the result as PNG. You can also add the cutout as a new layer and continue editing before the final export.

The tool uses a portrait segmentation model designed for people. It is not presented as a general product, animal or logo background remover. Clear light, a sharp subject and visible contrast normally improve the edge around hair and clothing.
