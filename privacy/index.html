Privacy Policy for Browser Image Editing

Last updated: 22 September 2026

The short version

The image you select is processed locally in your browser. PixEdito does not upload that selected image to its own server, create a cloud copy or require an account.

Images selected in the editor

Browser technologies read and manipulate the selected image on your device. Closing or refreshing the page clears the active editing session. Downloaded results are saved through your browser.

AI background remover

PixEdito’s single AI function removes the background from a person or portrait. It uses Google’s open-source MediaPipe Tasks runtime and a local portrait model. These software files download when needed; the selected image is processed on your device and is not sent to PixEdito or Google for image processing.

MediaPipe documentation states that Tasks SDKs may send performance and utilisation metrics to Google. Those technical diagnostics do not include the selected image. The AI feature is not used for blur, colour replacement, general photo enhancement or object removal.

Technical website information

Hosting and security providers may process routine request information such as IP address, browser type, requested page, timestamp and diagnostic logs to deliver and protect the website.

Analytics and advertising

PixEdito may use privacy-respecting analytics or display advertising. If cookies, local storage or similar identifiers are introduced, this policy will be updated and consent choices will be provided where required.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact page.
