Honest workflow

AI creates the cutout; layers create the new scene.

PixEdito has one AI action: remove a portrait background. Replacing that background is a normal layer-based editing task. This approach keeps the steps understandable and gives you control over the new colour, image, size and placement.

A transparent portrait can sit over a white background for a profile card, a brand colour for a team directory, or a photograph for a poster. Because the new background is a separate layer, you can move it, resize it, edit its colour and replace it without running the AI again.

The selected portrait is processed in your browser. Once the transparent result is added as a layer, use the editor’s ordinary file, layer, colour and transform controls to finish the composition.

Step by step

How to replace a portrait background

  1. 01

    Open the portrait

    Load the original photo into the PixEdito workspace.

  2. 02

    Remove the existing background

    Open Remove Background, use the current canvas and wait for the transparent preview.

  3. 03

    Add the portrait as a layer

    Add the cutout to the editor and keep it above the background layers.

  4. 04

    Create or import the new background

    Add a filled layer for a solid colour, or import another image and place it below the portrait.

  5. 05

    Arrange and export

    Resize and position the layers, check the edges, then save the final PNG, JPG or WebP file.

Better compositions

Background replacement tips

  • Match light direction between portrait and scene.
  • Use a simple background near detailed hair.
  • Keep enough contrast between clothing and colour.
  • Leave space for text before final export.
FAQ

Change background questions

Is background replacement an AI mode?

No. AI removes the portrait background; the replacement is created manually with layers.

Can I use a white background?

Yes. Add a white filled layer below the transparent portrait.

Can I use my own image?

Yes. Import it into the editor, place it below the portrait and resize it to cover the canvas.
