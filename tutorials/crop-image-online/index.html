How to crop an image online for free

Reframe a photo, remove distracting edges and choose the right aspect ratio without installing software.

Cropping changes which part of an image remains visible. It is useful when a photo includes distracting edges, the subject feels too small or you need a particular shape for a website or social platform.

PixEdito performs the crop in your browser. Your selected image does not need to be uploaded to our server before you can edit and download it.

Your image stays local

The selected file is processed inside your browser by the editor. It is not uploaded to an PixEdito server.

Step by step

How to do it

  1. 01

    Choose your image

    Open the editor and select a JPG, PNG or WebP file from your device. You can also drag the image into the upload area.

  2. 02

    Open the crop tool

    The Adjust tab opens first. Select Crop, then drag the crop handles until the frame contains exactly what you want to keep.

  3. 03

    Choose an aspect ratio

    Keep the crop freeform or select a preset ratio. A 1:1 ratio creates a square, 4:5 works well for portrait posts and 16:9 creates a wide landscape frame.

  4. 04

    Apply and check the result

    Apply the crop and use the comparison or undo controls if the subject feels too tight. Leave a little breathing room around faces and text.

  5. 05

    Download the cropped image

    Select Save, choose PNG, JPG or WebP, adjust the quality if needed and download the finished file to your device.

Better results

Useful tips

  • Keep the original file until you are happy with the crop.
  • Avoid cutting through joints when cropping people.
  • Use a fixed ratio when the image must fit a known platform slot.
Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

Does cropping reduce image quality?

Cropping removes pixels outside the selected frame, so the remaining image has smaller dimensions. Avoid enlarging a very tight crop beyond its remaining resolution.

Can I undo a crop?

Yes. Use Undo while the editor remains open. Keeping your original image also gives you a clean starting point later.
