One clear purpose

A background remover built into a complete image editor.

An AI background remover identifies the foreground subject and turns the remaining pixels transparent. PixEdito’s current model is designed specifically for people and portrait photos. That narrow purpose keeps the experience simple: upload a picture, wait for the cutout, compare the original and transparent result, then download or continue editing.

The processing happens in the browser. PixEdito downloads the MediaPipe runtime and portrait model only when the tool is opened. The selected photo is analysed on the device instead of being submitted to a PixEdito image-processing server. This makes the tool useful for profile photos, speaker portraits, team graphics, thumbnails, presentations and other work where a person needs to be separated from the scene.

Background removal is PixEdito’s only AI action. Crop, resize, text, drawing, selections, filters and file export remain ordinary editor tools. This separation makes it clear which result comes from the portrait model and which adjustments you control manually.

Step by step

How to remove a photo background

  1. 01

    Open the background remover

    Select “Remove Background” in the editor or use the button on this page. The model does not load until the tool is requested.

  2. 02

    Choose a portrait picture

    Open a JPG, PNG or WebP file. A sharp, well-lit photo with a clearly visible person normally gives the strongest outline.

  3. 03

    Wait for automatic removal

    PixEdito detects the person and creates the transparent result automatically. There are no extra AI modes to select.

  4. 04

    Inspect the transparent preview

    Review hair, shoulders, hands and clothing. A checkerboard behind the subject represents transparent pixels.

  5. 05

    Download or add a layer

    Download a transparent PNG, or add the cutout as a new layer and continue with PixEdito’s manual editing tools.

Better cutouts

Tips for cleaner portrait edges

  • Use a high-resolution photo without strong motion blur.
  • Choose a background that contrasts with hair and clothing.
  • Keep the complete head and shoulders inside the frame.
  • Avoid crowded group photos for the first attempt.
  • Keep the original file for any manual edge correction.
Quick answers

AI background remover FAQ

Is the PixEdito background remover free?

Yes. It is free to use without an account, subscription or API key.

Is my portrait uploaded?

No. PixEdito’s background removal runs in your browser after the model files download.

Can I save a transparent background?

Yes. Download the result as PNG, which preserves transparent pixels.

Does it remove backgrounds from products or pets?

The current model is intended for people and portraits. It does not promise reliable product, animal, logo or complex group cutouts.

Can I edit the cutout afterward?

Yes. Add it as a new layer to crop, resize, add text, draw or combine it with other layers.
