About PixEdito

Image editing should feel useful—not intimidating.

We built a focused editing space for everyday work: quick changes, clear guidance and no account standing between you and the result.

Our mission

A practical editor that respects your time and your files.

Many people do not need a large design suite. They need to crop a product photo, resize an image for social media, correct brightness, add a label or export a different format—and then get on with their day.

PixEdito brings those jobs together in one browser-based workspace. The editing engine runs on your device, so the image you select is not sent to an PixEdito server for processing.

The site is free because broad access comes first. Future advertising may support hosting, maintenance and new tutorials, but the core editor is intended to remain available without an account.

Private by default

Selected images are processed locally in the browser, not stored in an account.

Fast to understand

The editor is placed first, with focused tools and no forced onboarding.

Genuinely useful

Tutorials connect real tasks to the exact controls needed to complete them.

Open-source foundation

Built on transparent, proven tools.

The layered editing workspace uses miniPaint by ViliusL, provided under the MIT licence.
