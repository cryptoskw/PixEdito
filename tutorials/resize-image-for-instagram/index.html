How to resize an image for Instagram

Prepare square, portrait and landscape posts while keeping faces, products and text inside the safe frame.

Instagram supports several post shapes, but the composition still needs to work in the feed and profile grid. Choosing the shape before adding text or a watermark prevents important content from sitting too close to an edge.

For most feed posts, square and portrait layouts are the easiest starting points. Portrait posts occupy more vertical screen space, while square posts remain predictable across the grid.

Your image stays local

The selected file is processed inside your browser by the editor. It is not uploaded to an PixEdito server.

Step by step

How to do it

  1. 01

    Choose the post shape

    Use 1:1 for square, 4:5 for portrait or 1.91:1 for a wide landscape image. If you are unsure, begin with a square or 4:5 portrait crop.

  2. 02

    Crop around the subject

    Place faces, products and key actions away from the extreme edges. Keep enough space for the composition to breathe.

  3. 03

    Resize to practical pixels

    A common working width is 1080 pixels. Use 1080×1080 for square or 1080×1350 for a 4:5 portrait post.

  4. 04

    Check added text

    If your design includes text, view it at phone size. Increase contrast and avoid thin lettering that becomes difficult to read.

  5. 05

    Export and review

    Save as a high-quality JPG for a photograph or PNG for a graphic that needs crisp text. Open the downloaded file once before posting.

Better results

Useful tips

  • Keep important elements near the centre of the composition.
  • Do not repeatedly download and re-edit compressed copies.
  • Use one consistent ratio across a carousel.
Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

What is the best size for an Instagram portrait post?

A practical size is 1080×1350 pixels, which uses a 4:5 aspect ratio.

Should I use PNG or JPG for Instagram?

JPG is usually appropriate for photos. PNG can be useful for graphics or text-heavy designs, although the platform may still process the upload.
