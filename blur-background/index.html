Honest workflow

AI removes the background; the editor creates the blur.

PixEdito has one AI function: portrait background removal. Background blur is created with the normal layered editor. This gives you more control because the person and original photo remain separate layers. You can change the blur, reposition the cutout, crop the composition and add text without asking an AI model to make unrelated decisions.

The workflow is useful when a portrait was already taken against a busy room, street or event scene. A softer background reduces visual competition and can help the face stand out. It does not repair a badly focused subject, so begin with the sharpest portrait available.

Your portrait cutout is generated in the browser. After you add it as a new layer, select the original photo underneath and use the editor’s blur effect. The final file can be exported as PNG, JPG or WebP.

Step by step

How to blur the background

  1. 01

    Open the original photo

    Load the portrait into the PixEdito editor and keep it as the bottom layer.

  2. 02

    Remove the portrait background

    Open Remove Background and use the current canvas. Wait for the transparent preview.

  3. 03

    Add the cutout as a new layer

    The transparent person layer should sit above the original photo.

  4. 04

    Blur the original layer

    Select the bottom photo layer and apply a blur effect. Increase it gradually so the background softens without looking artificial.

  5. 05

    Review and export

    Check the portrait edges, crop if needed and save the finished image.

Natural results

Background blur tips

  • Keep the face and eyes sharp.
  • Use moderate blur for believable depth.
  • Check hair edges at 100% zoom.
  • Reduce distracting colour or brightness separately if needed.
FAQ

Blur background questions

Is blur an AI mode?

No. PixEdito’s AI only removes portrait backgrounds. Blur is applied manually to the original layer.

Can I change the blur later?

Use Undo while the session is open, or keep the source layers and repeat the effect at a different strength.

Does the photo upload to PixEdito?

No. The selected image and editing operations stay in your browser.
